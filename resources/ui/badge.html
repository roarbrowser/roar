The ladder

    How it works

    Your rank grows as you use Roar. The first three ranks come from the days you browse with Roar, no AI needed. From Hunter on, they also count the agent sessions and hours Roar sees when you connect a coding agent in Settings › Agents. Everything is counted on this computer and never leaves it unless you share your card.

    A rank you've reached stays yours: it never goes down, and Settings › Backup carries it to a new computer. Connect a coding agent
