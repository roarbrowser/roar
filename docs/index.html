Connect your agent

Claude Code

claude mcp add roar -- /Applications/Roar.app/Contents/Resources/roar-mcp.sh

Any MCP client takes the same command as a stdio server. It needs Node.js 22 or later, and starts Roar if it isn't running.

What's inside

What your agent gets

  • Real tabs. Pages the agent opens land in your sidebar, so you can watch, take over or close them.
  • A way to reach you. notify_user and ask_user put cards in the sidebar; ask_user waits for your click.
  • One-click cut-off. Click the dot in the sidebar to block agents from the space you're in. A setting closes the CDP port entirely, without a restart.
  • Headed or headless. Let agents use the Roar you see, or a hidden headless Roar with its own profile.
  • Only where you allow. Agents reach the spaces you allow; ghost tabs are always off-limits.
  • Looks like Chrome to sites. Roar reports the Chrome brand and a real window frame, so it trips fewer bot checks.

What you get

  • A vertical sidebar with spaces. Each space has its own tabs and its own profile.
  • Pinned tabs and tab groups, plus split view for two tabs side by side.
  • Tabs that archive themselves after 6h, 12h, 1 day or 1 week, like Arc did.
  • Command bar. ⌘T to open, search tabs, history and page text.
  • Reader mode and page to Markdown. Clean articles, or copy any page as Markdown.
  • Chrome extensions. uBlock Origin Lite comes bundled.
  • Leave Arc or Dia in one step. Your Arc spaces and pinned tabs come over on first launch; Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and Dia import too.

Roar vs the rest

The Browser Company put Arc in maintenance mode in May 2025 to build Dia. If you lived in Arc, a company decided for you that your browser was done. The new AI browsers ship their own assistant and keep the source closed. Roar is the other answer: MIT licensed, no built-in assistant, and your own agent plugged in over MCP.
