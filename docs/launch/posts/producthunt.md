## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt, I'm the maker of Roar.

The Browser Company put Arc in maintenance mode in May 2025 to build Dia, a closed-source browser with their AI built in. I wanted the opposite: a sidebar browser nobody can shelve, where the AI is the agent I already use.

Roar is that. Spaces with separate logins, pinned tabs, tabs that archive themselves, split view, a command bar, reader mode, Chrome extensions with uBlock Origin Lite. It also ships an MCP server, so Claude, Cursor or any MCP client can drive it. When an agent opens a page, it shows up as a tab in your sidebar. It can leave you a note or ask you a question with buttons and wait for your answer. You choose which spaces agents can reach, and one click on the sidebar dot blocks them from the space you're in.

Your Arc spaces and pinned tabs come over on first launch. It's free and MIT licensed. macOS only and early, so bug reports and feature requests on GitHub are very welcome: https://github.com/roarbrowser/roar
