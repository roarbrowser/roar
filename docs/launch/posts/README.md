- [ ] Demo video recorded and uploaded; replace `[VIDEO]` and link it in every post
- [x] Release DMG on GitHub Releases; Arc import shipped
- [ ] Re-read every claim against the shipped build
- [ ] Post from established accounts with real history, not fresh ones
- [ ] Show HN: Tuesday to Thursday, morning US Eastern time; post the first comment right away
- [ ] Stay around for the first few hours and answer every comment, including critical ones
- [ ] Don't ask anyone to upvote (HN and Reddit both penalize vote rings)
- [ ] Stagger channels instead of posting everything in one hour

## Community rules that affect posting

**Hacker News.** Show HN is for things people can try now; the repo works. Title in plain words, no superlatives. Don't solicit votes or comments.

**r/macapps.** Rules as seen via mirrors (check the live sidebar before posting):
- Developers who don't qualify for the main feed through the "Trust or Transparency" rule must post in the monthly "The App Pile" megathread. Apps not on the Mac App Store fall in that group unless they qualify through transparency; Roar being open source may qualify, so read the rule's explanation or ask the mods.
- Megathread posts use the PCP format: Problem, Comparison (1–2 competitors), Pricing. The draft follows it.
- Promotion counts toward a 30-day limit (Rule 3): one promo post per app per 30 days.
- Reddit auto-removes the post if the account's email isn't verified and the first comment in the sub contains a link.

**r/ArcBrowser.** I could not retrieve the official rules (Reddit blocked direct access and searches found none). The sub is unofficial (not run by The Browser Company), has a Self-Promotion flair, and general Reddit norms apply: disclose you're the developer, use the flair, keep your own posts to a small share of your activity there. Read the sidebar or message the mods before posting.

**Product Hunt.** Launch at 12:01 am Pacific; the maker comment goes up at launch.

**awesome-mcp-servers.** Submit a PR adding one line in alphabetical order under Browser Automation; follow its CONTRIBUTING notes.
