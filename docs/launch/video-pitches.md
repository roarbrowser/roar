# Hero video: three pitches and the verdict

The brief, given to three independent agencies that hadn't seen any earlier script: one landing-page hero video, 16:9, 30-60 s, built to be shared, spoken voiceover (no rap), only shipped features, no other company's brand or UI. Each agency worked only from the README, the release notes and the landing page.

## A. Halftone: "The Leash" (48 s)

- **Hook:** Your AI agent drives your browser, and you can cut it off with one click.
- **For:** developers and power users who already run an MCP agent and are uneasy about letting it loose in a browser.
- **Why it spreads:** at 0:28 the agent's next `new_page` fails with a red error while the music drops out. As a 6-second clip it answers the first objection on every agent-browser thread.
- **Arc:** a tab opens by itself, the agent reads and clicks, it asks, ghost tabs stay hidden from `list_pages`, one click cuts it off, a second click lets it back in, then a session row.
- **Music:** half-time trap, 140 bpm, F minor.

## B. Northpaw & Co.: "It Asks First" (55 s)

- **Hook:** Let AI do your browsing chores, and stay the one in charge.
- **For:** everyday AI-chat users who have never heard of MCP.
- **Why it spreads:** a question card ("Lisbon in May or September?") answered with one tap, then the one-click cut-off. People share the tech they wish they had.
- **Arc:** a trip to Lisbon gets planned in Wikipedia tabs, the agent asks, then says it's done, a ghost tab stays private, one click locks it out. It hedges honestly: "when it's your call, it asks".
- **Music:** warm funk, 96 bpm, D major.

## C. Signal/Noise: "A door you can lock" (48 s)

- **Hook:** Every browser is adding AI. This one comes with a door you can lock.
- **For:** developers with an agent, people wary of closed AI browsers, and the people who repost a clip of an agent being refused.
- **Why it spreads:** "Now watch this dot." Click, silence, red error, "No." It works with the sound off and loops.
- **Arc:** bring your own agent, real tabs, a ghost tab it can't list, then the dot, then it asks and reports.
- **Music:** funk, 96 bpm, E minor, with a hard cut to silence at the click.

## Client-side verdict: a merge

- **Opening and structure from Halftone.** A tab opening by itself is the strongest first 3 seconds and needs no explanation. Signal/Noise opens on a claim before showing anything.
- **The shareable beat from Signal/Noise:** "Now watch this dot." The TEMP voice can't hold a dramatic silence, so the music drop and the refusal stab carry it, and the line becomes "One click. Blocked."
- **Northpaw's wording for the question beat**, because the agent, not Roar, decides when to ask.
- **Cut:** Halftone's session row (it needs a logged-in CLI and shows a third-party name) and its split view (a capture risk).
- **The old scripts lose.** They're rap, and the developer cut depends on a logged-in CLI session.

The result is [demo-script.md](demo-script.md): 50 s, funk at 96 bpm in E minor, end card "Bring your agent. Keep control."

**Everyday cut:** keep it as a second video. It should be rebuilt from Northpaw's Lisbon script with spoken VO and framing that is friendlier to non-developers. Until then, [demo-script-everyday.md](demo-script-everyday.md) stays as it is.
