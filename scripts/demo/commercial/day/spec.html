Design / Onboarding

Onboarding spec v3

Owned by Design Review. Updated today. 6 open comments.

New people should reach their first useful result in under two minutes. The welcome screen asks one question, then gets out of the way.

Import

Step one brings over bookmarks and saved logins from the browser they already use. It runs in the background so nobody waits on a progress bar.

First pins

Step two offers to pin the three sites they open most, guessed from their history. Every suggestion can be skipped, and nothing is required.

How we measure it

We count how many people finish setup and come back the next day, not how many screens they see. Anything that doesn't move that number is cut.

Open question

Should import pause while the person is typing in the first tab? Daniel thinks yes; engineering wants data first.
